The problem

Every banking action is a stack of screens, taps and forms.

So we bank only when we can stop and stare at a phone. Hands wet, eyes on the road, a child on your hip, and the bank still asks you to read a menu and fill a field.

Menus assume reading

Nobody wants to browse a bank. You want to ask a question and get an answer.

Forms assume typing

An IBAN field needs both hands and all your attention. It is where the conversation ends.

Confirmations assume doubt

Under pressure anyone taps yes. Another tap changes nothing.

The product

One mic tap. It listens, answers out loud, and keeps listening.

The voice carries the gist. The card makes every answer glanceable the moment you do look down. Type instead any time, and the whole app flips to right-to-left Urdu when you want it to.

Why this is safe to put in a bank

The assistant can do everything the bank does. It cannot move a single rupee.

It holds the user’s own token, calls the same public API as the app, and has no database access. Every write it makes is a proposal the bank core still has to run.

Step 01 · Prepare

The assistant proposes

A write tool creates a pending action and nothing else. Money has not moved.

Step 02 · PIN

The user confirms

A confirmation card and the PIN sheet open in the conversation. The assistant never sees the PIN.

Step 03 · Execute once

The core disposes

One atomic, idempotent, single-use call. Unexecuted actions expire in two minutes.

Guardian approval

A trusted contact approves large or unusual sends from their own phone, with their own PIN.

Scam check-in

One question before a risky send: did someone call and ask you to send this? Yes cancels it, calmly.

Cooling period

Tightening is instant. Removing a guardian or raising the ceiling cools off first, and the outgoing guardian keeps authority until it lands.

The model is allowed to be creative about words, never about actions.

What this is not yet

Everything above is real. Here is exactly what is not.

  • The world is seeded. Balances, titles, bills and card numbers are deterministic fakes.
  • The institutions are simulated. No rails, no settlement ledger, no KYC, no real SMS.
  • One platform. Verified on the iOS simulator; Android has not been run in weeks.
  • Urdu trails English by a turn on two flows, and routing stays probabilistic.

All of it is visible in the repo.
